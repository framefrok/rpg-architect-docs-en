# Battle Log

## **Battle Log**[¶](#battle-log "Permanent link")

Battle Log elements add a message entry to the battle log.

> **Note**: For example, \[Enemy\] uses \[Item\]!
> 
> **Note**: The Battle Log must be enabled for battle log messages to appear.
