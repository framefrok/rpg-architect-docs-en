# Exit Vehicle

## **Exit Vehicle**[¶](#exit-vehicle "Permanent link")

Exits the party from their current vehicle, optionally running the vehicle's exit scripts and teleporting the party afterward.

## **Properties**[¶](#properties "Permanent link")

#### **System**[¶](#system "Permanent link")

Name

Explanation

Type

Direction

The direction the party faces after teleporting. When left empty, the current direction is kept.

[Direction](../../../05-reference/direction/)

Map ID

The ID of the map to teleport to after exiting.

[Variable or Value](../../../05-reference/variable-or-value/)

Run Scripts

Whether to run the vehicle's before and after exit scripts.

Toggle

Teleport

Whether to teleport the party to a map and position after exiting the vehicle.

Toggle

X

The X coordinate to teleport to after exiting.

[Variable or Value](../../../05-reference/variable-or-value/)

Y

The Y coordinate to teleport to after exiting.

[Variable or Value](../../../05-reference/variable-or-value/)

Z

The Z coordinate to teleport to after exiting.

[Variable or Value](../../../05-reference/variable-or-value/)
