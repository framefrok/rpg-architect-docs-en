# Switch

## **Switch**[¶](#switch "Permanent link")

Switches store boolean (true/false) values used to track states and toggles in the game, such as whether a door is unlocked or a quest is complete.

> **Note**: Switches exist in two scopes: **Global** switches persist throughout the entire game, while **Local** switches belong to individual entities and are independent per instance. See [Local and Global Data](../local-and-global-data/) for details.
