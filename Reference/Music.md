# Music

## **Music**[¶](#music "Permanent link")

RPG Architect supports many [Audio Formats](../audio/).

Music is largely different than [Sound Effects](../sound-effect/), in that generally only one can be played at a time. Additional background effects can be played with the [Play Background Effect](../../07-commands/03-audio/10-play-background-effect/) command, which operate the same as music.
