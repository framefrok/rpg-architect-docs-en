# Sound Effect

## **Sound Effect**[¶](#sound-effect "Permanent link")

RPG Architect supports the following audio formats for sound effects:

*   **WAV** (.wav) — Uncompressed audio, best for short sound effects
*   **OGG Vorbis** (.ogg) — Compressed audio, good for longer effects
*   **MP3** (.mp3) — Compressed audio

Sound effects are one-shot audio clips that play once and stop. For looping ambient audio, use [Background Effects](../../07-commands/03-audio/10-play-background-effect/). For music tracks, use [Music](../music/).
